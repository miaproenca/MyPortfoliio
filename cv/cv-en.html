Alerta Enchentes RS

Alerta Enchentes RS

Real-time flood monitoring app

2024 · With Gabriela Munhoz · Academic project

A free app concept that helps people in flood-prone areas of Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil, follow river levels and rainfall in real time and reach rescue teams.

  • Two surveys, with 46 and 47 people: 62% had received guidance on how to act, but only 25% found the available information sufficient.
  • Designed two flows, for residents and for rescue teams, including a risk map and an SOS button.
  • Delivered a visual identity, a style guide and high-fidelity prototypes.
